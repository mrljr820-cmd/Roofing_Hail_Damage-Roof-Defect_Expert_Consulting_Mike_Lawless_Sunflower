A Closer Look at Ice Ball Impact Testing
and the True Resultant Velocity of Real-Life Hail Stones

In this detailed research paper, I expose critical flaws in the laboratory "ice ball" testing methods commonly used by insurance companies and their engineers to deny legitimate hail damage claims.

Lab tests use perfect spheres — real hail is irregular and random in shape.
They test in "vacuum free-fall" from only 84–259 feet — ignoring real storm dynamics.
Perpendicular impacts only — impossible on sloped roofs in real storms.
Real hail produces "resultant velocities" from storm motion + surface wind gusts, creating significantly higher kinetic energy.

My calculations include precise volumes, masses (1" hail ≈ 7.87g to 3" hail ≈ 212g), and the physics of how wind-driven hail actually strikes roofs.
